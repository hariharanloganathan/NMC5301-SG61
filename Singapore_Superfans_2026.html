05 · Conclusion

Three key insights

What this case teaches about destination marketing.

01

Attention needs an action

A recognisable icon can redirect attention, but changing behaviour requires a clear action.

02

Transparency earns trust

Participatory campaigns need transparent rules, judging criteria and visible recognition to earn trust.

03

Measure real exploration

Engagement and promotional bookings are not proof of tourism success. Future campaigns should measure actual destination exploration and distinguish overseas prospects from visitors already in Singapore.

The next stage of destination marketing should not just encourage travellers to post about Singapore. It should give them a reason to experience it.
